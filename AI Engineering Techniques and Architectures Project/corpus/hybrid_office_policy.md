# POL-017: Hybrid Office, Workspace, and Visitor Policy

**Owner:** Facilities and People Operations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees working from a Northwind office

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Northwind's offices in Austin, Karachi, and London are used, how desks and rooms are booked, and what is expected when employees and visitors are on site. It supports the hybrid work arrangement in POL-003, under which hybrid employees work from their assigned office at least 3 days per week.

## 2. Office Hours and Access

Offices are open from 7:00 to 19:00 on working days. Employees use their badge to enter and must not allow another person to follow them in without a badge ("tailgating"). Access outside office hours must be approved by the manager and Facilities. Badges that are lost must be reported to Facilities immediately, and in any case within 24 hours, so that they can be deactivated.

## 3. Team Core Days and Desk Booking

Each team uses Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday as in-office core days. Hybrid employees choose the remaining day or days with their manager. Desks are not assigned permanently; employees book a desk through the facilities portal up to 14 days ahead and release the booking if their plans change. Bookings that are not checked in by 10:30 are released after a reminder. Fully remote employees who visit an office for a planned event can book a desk in the same way.

## 4. Meeting Rooms

Meeting rooms can be booked through the calendar system. Rooms that are booked but not used within 10 minutes of the start time are released. For meetings with remote participants, the organizer must use the video-conference equipment in the room and make sure that remote participants can see and hear everyone.

## 5. Visitors and Guests

Visitors must be registered in advance by the host, sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge, and be accompanied at all times. Visitors may not enter areas where Confidential or Restricted information is displayed or discussed (see POL-004). Family members who visit the office for an approved event must be registered in the same way. Children may not be left unattended.

## 6. Clean Desk and Shared Spaces

Employees should leave desks clear at the end of the day, lock Confidential and Restricted documents away, and not leave laptops unattended. Kitchen areas must be kept clean. Food with strong smells should not be eaten at desks. Quiet rooms and lactation rooms (see POL-016) are available for rest, prayer, and private calls and must be left tidy.

## 7. Closures and Severe Weather

If an office has to close because of severe weather, a power failure, or another emergency, the People team will send a message through email and the emergency notification system as early as possible. Employees who cannot reach the office because of the weather should work from home if they can do so safely; they will not be asked to use PTO for a closure that Northwind announces. Non-exempt employees are paid for scheduled hours on days that the company closes an office unexpectedly, except where a closure is caused by the employee's own situation.

## 8. Commuting and Parking

Parking in Austin is first-come, first-served in the visitor-marked areas. Employees can ask for a transit pass in Karachi and London through People Operations, which is paid by Northwind up to the limits in the local transport benefit. Commuting costs are not reimbursable as business expenses (see POL-005).

## 9. Health and Safety on Site

Employees must follow safety signs, keep walkways clear, and report hazards or injuries as required by POL-016. In the event of a fire alarm, everyone must leave the building and meet at the assembly point. Weapons are not allowed on any Northwind premises.

## 10. Feedback

Employees who have suggestions about the offices can send them to facilities@northwind.example or raise them with their manager.
