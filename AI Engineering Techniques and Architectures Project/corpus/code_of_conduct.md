# POL-008: Code of Conduct and Workplace Behavior Policy

**Owner:** People Operations — Employee Relations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees, contractors, vendors, and visitors

## 1. Purpose

Northwind expects everyone to treat one another with respect, honesty, and professionalism. This policy sets out the standards of behavior at work, how to report concerns, and how Northwind handles those concerns. It applies in the office, remotely, at company events, on business travel, and in online communications that relate to work.

## 2. Expected Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, to follow the law and company policies, to respect colleagues and customers, to protect company assets and data, and to speak up when something seems wrong. Managers have additional responsibilities: they must model good conduct, respond promptly when someone raises a concern, and never ignore a report.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The following are not tolerated:

- **Harassment**, including sexual harassment, bullying, intimidation, offensive jokes, and unwelcome comments or contact;
- **Discrimination** based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, pregnancy, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic;
- **Retaliation** against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation;
- **Workplace violence**, threats, or possession of weapons on company premises;
- **Working while impaired** by alcohol or drugs; and
- **Dishonesty**, such as falsifying records, time reports, or expense claims.

## 4. How to Report a Concern

Employees can report a concern through any of these channels. They do not need to go to their manager first.

1. Their direct manager or any other manager they trust;
2. Their HR Business Partner;
3. The **Employee Relations** team at employeerelations@northwind.example; or
4. The **Ethics Hotline**, which is run by an independent third party, is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and accepts anonymous reports.

If someone is in immediate danger, they should call local emergency services first and then notify Northwind.

## 5. Investigation Process

When a concern is reported to Employee Relations:

- Employee Relations **acknowledges the report within 2 business days**.
- An investigator is assigned and the investigation **begins within 5 business days**.
- Investigations are aimed at being **completed within 30 calendar days**. More complex cases may take longer, and the people involved will be updated.
- Information is kept confidential to the extent possible and is shared only with people who need to know to carry out the investigation or take action.
- Both the person who raised the concern and the person it is about are told the outcome, to the extent that is appropriate.

Investigations are carried out only by trained Employee Relations staff or approved external investigators. Managers must not investigate harassment or discrimination complaints on their own. They must refer them to Employee Relations promptly, normally within 1 business day.

## 6. Non-Retaliation

Northwind prohibits retaliation against any employee who, in good faith, raises a concern, asks a question about this policy, or helps with an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation and may result in termination. An employee who believes they are being retaliated against should contact Employee Relations or the Ethics Hotline immediately.

## 7. Matters That Must Be Escalated to Employee Relations

The following matters must always be referred to Employee Relations or Legal and must not be decided by a manager, by a self-service tool, or by an automated HR assistant:

- allegations of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or bullying;
- safety threats or workplace violence;
- legal threats, subpoenas, or contact from regulators;
- requests for accommodation related to disability; and
- allegations of fraud or serious misconduct.

An automated HR assistant may explain how and where to report these matters and may create a case summary for Employee Relations with the employee's consent. It must not give legal advice or decide whether a policy was violated.

## 8. Conflicts of Interest and Outside Work

Employees must disclose any situation in which a personal interest could conflict with Northwind's interests, such as a family member working for a competitor or supplier. Outside employment is allowed with written approval from the manager, provided that it does not compete with Northwind, use company resources, or interfere with job performance.

## 9. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees must not give or accept gifts, meals, or entertainment that could influence a business decision. Gifts with a value of more than **$100** must be declined or reported to the manager and Finance. Cash and cash equivalents are never allowed. Gifts to or from government officials must be pre-approved by Legal.

## 10. Corrective Action

Violations of this policy may lead to corrective action that can include coaching, a written warning, a final warning, suspension, or termination. Northwind usually uses progressive steps but may go directly to termination for serious misconduct.
