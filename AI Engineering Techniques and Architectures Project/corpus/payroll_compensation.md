# POL-014: Payroll and Compensation Policy

**Owner:** Finance — Payroll and People Operations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how and when Northwind pays its employees, how pay changes are made, and what employees should do if they find a mistake in their pay. Expense reimbursement is covered separately in POL-005 and is paid on a different schedule from payroll.

## 2. Pay Schedule

- **United States:** employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business day of each month.
- **Pakistan (Karachi):** employees are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month.
- **United Kingdom (London):** employees are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month.

If a pay date falls on a weekend or a company holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day. Pay is paid by direct deposit to the bank account that the employee has registered in the HR system. Employees who change bank details must submit the change at least **5 business days** before the pay date for the change to take effect on that pay date. For security reasons, bank details are changed only through the HR system and never by email or chat.

## 3. Payroll Cut-Off and Timekeeping

Non-exempt employees must submit and get approval for their time records by the Monday after the end of the pay period. Managers must approve time records within 2 business days. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular rate (see POL-012). Time records that are submitted after the cut-off are paid in the next pay period.

## 4. Compensation Reviews and Changes

Compensation is reviewed once a year, and any changes normally take effect on March 1. Promotions and role changes can be made at other times with approval from the department VP and the People team. Pay changes are communicated in writing before they take effect. Employees are asked not to discuss other people's pay, but Northwind does not prohibit employees from discussing their own pay, and nothing in this policy limits rights protected by law.

## 5. Bonuses and Incentives

Eligibility for a bonus or incentive plan is set out in the offer letter or the plan document. Unless a plan says otherwise, an employee must be employed on the payout date to receive a bonus. Bonuses for part of a year are pro-rated by the number of months worked.

## 6. Taxes and Deductions

Northwind withholds payroll taxes and other deductions as required by law and as chosen by the employee, such as benefit premiums and 401(k) contributions. U.S. employees complete a Form W-4 when they join and can update it in the HR system at any time. Employees who work temporarily from another U.S. state or from another country may cause changes in tax withholding, as described in POL-003.

## 7. Pay Errors

Employees who believe that they have been underpaid or overpaid must tell Payroll through a ticket in the HR system as soon as possible and no later than 30 days after the pay date. Underpayments of more than $100 that are confirmed will be corrected in an off-cycle payment within **3 business days**. Smaller underpayments are corrected in the next regular pay period. Overpayments will be recovered after the employee is told in writing, and in line with local law.

## 8. Final Pay

On termination, final pay, including any unpaid wages and any PTO that must be paid out under local law (see POL-001, Section 9), is paid on or before the date required by the law of the employee's work location. Employees must return company equipment under POL-010.

## 9. Pay Statements and Employment Records

Pay statements are available in the HR system. Employees can ask People Operations for a copy of their records. Employment and income verification letters are handled as described in POL-012.
