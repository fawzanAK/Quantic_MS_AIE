# POL-005: Expense and Reimbursement Policy

**Owner:** Finance | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees

## 1. Principles

Northwind reimburses reasonable, necessary business expenses that are incurred in the course of work and are supported by receipts. Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own. Expenses must be approved by the right person, submitted on time, and recorded accurately. Questions can be sent to the Finance team at expenses@northwind.example.

## 2. Submission Rules

- **Deadline:** submit an expense report within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted later than 30 days may be denied.
- **Receipts:** an itemized receipt is required for every expense of more than $25 (or the local-currency equivalent). For expenses of $25 or less, a short description is enough.
- **Reimbursement timing:** approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days of final approval, to the employee's payroll bank account.
- **Currency:** expenses in other currencies are converted at the rate on the transaction date. Employees in Pakistan and the United Kingdom must include tax receipts (such as VAT) where applicable.

## 3. Approval Limits

An expense report must be approved by the following person based on the total amount of the report:

| Total of report | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $500.01 to $2,500 | Department Director |
| Over $2,500 | Department VP (and Finance review) |

Employees may not approve their own expenses or expenses of someone who reports to them if the approval would create a conflict of interest. Items that need pre-approval under this policy must be approved **before** the money is spent.

## 4. Meals and Entertainment

While on approved business travel, meals are reimbursed up to a **per diem of $75 per day**. Meals during a normal workday at home or in the office are not reimbursed. Client entertainment must be approved in advance by a Director and is limited to $100 per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable except for up to two drinks per person at pre-approved client entertainment.

## 5. Lodging, Airfare, and Ground Transportation

- **Lodging:** up to $250 per night, or $325 per night in designated high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, and Tokyo).
- **Airfare:** economy class for flights of 6 hours or less; premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours. Business and first class are not reimbursable. See POL-011 for booking rules.
- **Ground transportation:** taxis and rideshares are reimbursable; rental cars should be compact or mid-size. Personal vehicle mileage within the United States is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile.
- **Pre-approval:** all travel must be approved before booking (see POL-011).

## 6. Home Office Stipend

To help employees set up a safe and comfortable workspace, Northwind provides a **one-time home office stipend**:

- **$400** for fully remote employees; and
- **$200** for hybrid employees.

The stipend can be used for an **ergonomic chair**, a desk or standing-desk converter, a monitor stand, task lighting, a keyboard, and a mouse. A chair purchased for the home office is therefore eligible, within the stipend amount. Office-based employees are not eligible.

**Conditions.** The employee must have completed the 90-day introductory period. The employee must submit an itemized receipt within 30 days of the purchase. The stipend is one time only; any amount above the limit is the employee's own cost. The stipend does not cover computers, laptops, tablets, phones, monitors, or software.

**Internet stipend.** Fully remote employees can claim up to $50 per month for home internet with a monthly receipt or bill.

## 7. Laptops, Computers, and Other Equipment

Laptops and other computing devices are provided by IT and **cannot be reimbursed** if an employee buys them personally. Employees who need a laptop or accessory should submit a request to IT under POL-010. A personally purchased laptop will not be reimbursed even when the cost is below the approval limits, because company data must be kept on managed devices (see POL-004). The only exception is a written pre-approval from the IT Director during a documented supply emergency.

## 8. Items That Are Never Reimbursable

Fines and penalties, personal travel or side trips, hotel or flight upgrades, personal grooming, entertainment that was not pre-approved, alcohol outside the exception in Section 4, gifts above the limit in POL-008, and any item purchased without a receipt where one is required.

## 9. Corporate Cards

Employees who have a corporate card must use it only for business expenses. Personal charges on a corporate card must be reported and repaid within 14 days.

## 10. Fraud and False Claims

Submitting false, altered, or duplicate expenses is a serious violation. It can lead to repayment of the amount, corrective action under POL-008, and termination. Anyone who suspects expense fraud can report it to Finance or to the Ethics Hotline described in POL-008.
