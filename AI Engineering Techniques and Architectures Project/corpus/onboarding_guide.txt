POL-009 | New Hire Onboarding Guide and Checklist
Owner: People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Applies to: All new employees and their managers
================================================================

SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND OVERVIEW

This guide explains what happens when a new employee joins Northwind Analytics and what the employee and manager should complete. A good start helps new hires feel welcome, productive, and secure. The first 90 calendar days of employment are the introductory period, which is described in POL-012.

SECTION 2. BEFORE THE FIRST DAY

The People team, IT, and the hiring manager complete these steps at least 5 business days before the start date:

  - Send the welcome email with the start date, start time, and a first-day agenda.
  - IT orders and configures the new hire's laptop and accessories and ships it so that it arrives at least 3 days before the start date for remote employees (see POL-010).
  - The manager assigns an onboarding buddy.
  - The manager creates a 30-60-90 day plan with the new hire's first projects.
  - The People team prepares employment paperwork, including local requirements for the United States, Pakistan, or the United Kingdom.

SECTION 3. DAY ONE CHECKLIST

  [ ] Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.
  [ ] Complete Section 1 of the U.S. Form I-9 (U.S. employees); Section 2, the document review, must be completed within 3 business days of the start date.
  [ ] Set up your accounts, including single sign-on and multi-factor authentication (see POL-004).
  [ ] Install required security software on your device and check that the VPN works.
  [ ] Submit payroll information, including direct deposit details, in the HR system.
  [ ] Review the team calendar and your first-week schedule.

SECTION 4. FIRST WEEK CHECKLIST

  [ ] Attend the company orientation session.
  [ ] Review the employee handbook, including POL-001 (PTO), POL-003 (remote work), POL-005 (expenses), and POL-008 (code of conduct).
  [ ] Meet the key people on your team and the departments you will work with.
  [ ] Set up your workspace. Remote and hybrid employees should read the home office stipend rules in POL-005 and note that the stipend is available only after the introductory period.
  [ ] Agree on your goals for the first 30 days with your manager.

SECTION 5. FIRST 30 DAYS

New hires must finish the following within the first 30 days unless a shorter deadline is stated:

  - **Compliance training:** security awareness training, code of conduct training, and harassment prevention training must be completed within 14 days of the start date.
  - **Benefits enrollment:** employees must enroll in benefits within 30 days of becoming eligible (see POL-006). Coverage for eligible full-time employees starts on the first day of the month after the start date.
  - **401(k):** eligible employees can start contributions after 30 days of employment.
  - **Manager check-in:** a one-on-one meeting each week with the manager during the first month.

SECTION 6. THE 30-60-90 DAY PLAN AND THE INTRODUCTORY PERIOD

The manager and new hire review progress at 30, 60, and 90 days. At 90 days, the manager completes an introductory-period review. The introductory period lasts 90 calendar days from the start date. During this time:

  - the employee accrues PTO from day one but cannot take it without People team approval (POL-001);
  - the employee is not eligible for temporary work from another state or country (POL-003);
  - the home office stipend cannot be claimed until the period is over (POL-005);
  - paid company holidays are available from the first day (POL-002); and
  - parental leave and similar leaves begin to be available after the period ends (POL-007).

SECTION 7. MANAGER ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

Managers should:

  1. Confirm the new hire's equipment request has been submitted at least 5 business days before the start date.
  2. Introduce the new hire to the team on the first day.
  3. Make sure the new hire has access to all required systems within the first 3 days.
  4. Hold a weekly one-on-one for the first 30 days.
  5. Complete the introductory-period review by day 90.

SECTION 8. OFFICES OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES

In Karachi and London, the People team provides local onboarding documents, such as the employment contract acknowledgement, tax forms, and the local health-insurance or pension enrollment forms. The first-day checklist is otherwise the same.

SECTION 9. QUESTIONS

New hires should ask their onboarding buddy first and then their HR Business Partner. For IT issues, contact the IT Service Desk.
