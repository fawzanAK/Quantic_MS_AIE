# POL-011: Business Travel Policy

**Owner:** Finance and People Operations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees who travel for Northwind business

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how business travel is approved, booked, and reimbursed. Reimbursement limits for meals, lodging, and flights are in POL-005. Information security rules for travel with devices are in POL-004 and POL-010.

## 2. Pre-Approval

All business travel must be approved **before** anything is booked.

- **Domestic travel:** approval from the employee's direct manager.
- **International travel:** approval from the department Director, in addition to the manager.
- **Travel costing more than $2,500 in total:** approval from the department VP.

Expenses for travel that was not pre-approved may be denied.

## 3. Booking

Employees must book flights, hotels, and rental cars through the Northwind travel portal so that the company can keep track of where its people are in an emergency and can get negotiated rates. Booking outside the portal is allowed only when the portal has no option, and the reason must be written on the expense report. Book at least 14 days before departure where possible.

## 4. Flights

Employees should book economy class for flights of **6 hours or less**. For flights longer than 6 hours, premium economy is allowed. Business class and first class are not reimbursed. Changes and upgrades requested for personal reasons are paid by the employee.

## 5. Hotels, Meals, and Ground Transportation

Hotel and meal limits follow POL-005, which allows up to $250 per night for lodging ($325 in designated high-cost cities) and a meal per diem of $75 per day. Employees should choose reasonably priced options that are close to the business location. Taxis, rideshares, and public transport are allowed. Rental cars should be compact or mid-size and should be returned with a full tank.

## 6. Visas, Insurance, and Health

Northwind pays for visas that are needed for business travel and provides global travel medical and emergency evacuation insurance for approved trips. Employees are responsible for having a valid passport (with at least six months of validity for most destinations) and for checking entry rules. Travel to any country that the government advisory ranks at Level 4 (Do Not Travel) is not allowed without approval from the CISO and the Chief People Officer.

## 7. Personal Time on Business Trips

Employees may add personal days before or after a business trip, provided that the manager approves and that the personal days are taken as PTO under POL-001. Any additional costs that result from the personal days, such as extra hotel nights or a changed flight, are paid by the employee. The extra days must not increase the total cost of the trip to Northwind.

## 8. Business Travel Compared With Working Remotely

Business travel at the company's request is different from temporary remote work that an employee asks for under POL-003. Time on approved business travel does not count toward the 20-business-day annual limit on temporary remote work. An employee who wants to extend a business trip in order to keep working from the destination for their own reasons must request that stay under POL-003.

## 9. Safety and Duty of Care

Travelers must keep their itinerary updated in the travel portal and have their phone available at all times. In an emergency, the traveler should contact local emergency services, then the Northwind emergency line, and then their manager. Northwind will provide help with medical evacuation and rebooking.

## 10. Expense Reports After Travel

Submit the expense report within 30 days of the end of the trip with itemized receipts for expenses of more than $25. Unused tickets must be reported and credited back to Northwind. See POL-005 for details of who approves the report.
