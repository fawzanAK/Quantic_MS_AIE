POL-012: Employment Types, Working Hours, and Introductory Period

Owner: People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Applies to: All Northwind workers

1. Employment Classifications

Northwind uses the following classifications. The classification is recorded in the HR system and determines which policies and benefits apply.

  • Full-time employee: scheduled to work at least 35 hours per week (normally 40). Full-time employees are eligible for PTO, sick leave, and the full benefits program.
  • Part-time employee: scheduled to work fewer than 35 hours per week. Part-time employees earn PTO and sick days in proportion to their scheduled hours. Those who work at least 20 hours per week are eligible for some benefits as described in POL-006.
  • Contractor: an independent worker who is not an employee. Contractors are paid under a service agreement, are responsible for their own taxes, and are not eligible for PTO, sick leave, company holidays, benefits, the home office stipend, or the expense policy unless their agreement says so.
  • Intern: a temporary employee in a fixed-term program, who is not eligible for PTO or benefits except where required by law.

2. Exempt and Non-Exempt Status (United States)

In the United States, employees are classified as exempt or non-exempt under wage-and-hour law. Non-exempt employees record their working hours and are paid overtime at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a work week. Overtime must be approved by the manager in advance. Exempt employees are paid a salary and do not receive overtime. Rules in Pakistan and the United Kingdom follow local law.

3. Working Hours and Core Hours

The standard work week is 40 hours for full-time employees. Teams set core hours, normally 10:00 to 15:00 in the team's main time zone, during which employees are expected to be available. Outside core hours, employees have flexibility in when they work. Non-exempt employees must take an unpaid meal break of at least 30 minutes for any shift longer than 6 hours, or as local law requires.

4. Introductory Period

The first 90 calendar days of employment are the introductory period. It is a time for the employee and manager to check that the role is a good match. During the introductory period:

  • Employees accrue PTO from day one but may not take PTO without written People team approval (POL-001, Section 2).
  • Employees may take paid company holidays (POL-002).
  • Employees are not eligible for temporary work from another state or country (POL-003, Section 3).
  • Employees cannot claim the home office stipend (POL-005, Section 6).
  • Employees cannot start paid parental leave until the period is completed (POL-007, Section 3).
  • Benefits start on the first day of the month after the start date (POL-006).

Completing the introductory period does not change the at-will nature of employment in the United States, or the notice periods in local law and contracts elsewhere.

5. Performance Reviews and Compensation

Northwind holds performance reviews twice a year, in May and November. Compensation changes are normally decided once a year in March. Managers hold regular one-on-one meetings with their team members and should give feedback throughout the year, not only at review time.

6. Internal Transfers and Promotions

An employee is normally eligible to apply for an internal transfer after 12 months in their current role, with the approval of both managers and the People team. Exceptions require the approval of the department VP. Employees in the introductory period cannot apply for transfers.

7. Resignation and Notice

Employees are asked to give at least two weeks' written notice when they resign, and Directors and above are asked to give four weeks. In Pakistan and the United Kingdom, contractual and statutory notice periods apply. Northwind may choose to end an employee's last day earlier and may pay in lieu of notice as local law allows.

8. Employment Verification

Employment and income verification requests from banks, landlords, and visa offices are handled by the People team. Employees should send requests to people@northwind.example. Northwind confirms only dates of employment and job title unless the employee gives written permission to share more.
