# POL-016: Workplace Health, Safety, and Employee Wellbeing Policy

**Owner:** People Operations and Facilities | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and visitors

## 1. Purpose

Northwind is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace and to supporting the wellbeing of its people. This policy covers workplace safety, injury reporting, workplace accommodations, and the employee assistance program (EAP). Leaves of absence are in POL-007, and benefits are in POL-006.

## 2. General Safety

Employees must follow safety rules in the office and when working at home, report hazards, and look out for one another. Every office has marked emergency exits, first-aid kits, and trained first-aid responders. New employees are shown the emergency procedures on their first day (POL-009). Fire drills take place at least once a year in each office.

## 3. Reporting Injuries and Incidents

Any work-related injury or illness, however minor, must be reported to the employee's manager and to People Operations **within 24 hours**. Northwind will complete the required incident report and, where required by law, will notify the relevant authority. In the United States, work-related injuries are covered by workers' compensation. Employees must not be discouraged from reporting an injury, and Northwind does not retaliate against employees who do so. Near misses should also be reported so that hazards can be fixed.

## 4. Home Office Safety

Employees who work from home are responsible for keeping a safe workspace, with working smoke detectors, safe electrical connections, and clear walkways. Free ergonomic assessments are available through IT and People Operations (see POL-010). The home office stipend in POL-005, Section 6, can be used to buy ergonomic items.

## 5. Workplace Accommodations

Northwind provides reasonable accommodations for employees with disabilities, for pregnancy and related conditions, and for sincerely held religious practices, unless this would cause undue hardship. To request an accommodation, an employee can contact their HR Business Partner or Employee Relations in writing or in person. The People team will begin the **interactive process** within **5 business days** and will aim to give a decision within **10 business days** of receiving the information it needs. Medical information that is provided to support a request is kept confidential and separate from the employee's personnel file. Accommodation requests that relate to a disability must be referred to Employee Relations, as stated in POL-008, Section 7.

## 6. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees, including part-time employees and their household members, can use the confidential Employee Assistance Program. The EAP offers up to **6 free counseling sessions per issue per year**, available 24 hours a day by phone and video, and also gives help with legal questions, financial planning, childcare, and eldercare. Use of the EAP is confidential, and Northwind does not receive the names of people who use it. In Pakistan and the United Kingdom, equivalent local services are available through the group health and medical insurance plans.

## 7. Lactation and Quiet Rooms

Offices have private rooms that are available for nursing parents and as a quiet space for rest or prayer. Employees can book these rooms through the facilities portal. Nursing parents who work remotely can ask their manager for flexibility in scheduling breaks.

## 8. Mental Health and Workload

Managers should watch for signs of excessive stress or workload and should talk to their team members about priorities. Employees who feel overwhelmed are encouraged to talk to their manager, to use the EAP, and to take their sick days or PTO when they need to (see POL-001 and POL-007). An employee in crisis should call local emergency services or a crisis line, and can then let their manager or the People team know.

## 9. Substance Use and Weapons

The use, possession, or sale of illegal drugs, and being under the influence of alcohol or drugs while at work, is prohibited, as is bringing weapons into a Northwind office. Responsible alcohol use at approved company events is allowed. Employees who struggle with substance use are encouraged to seek help through the EAP.

## 10. Emergency Contacts

Each employee should keep their emergency contact details up to date in the HR system. In an emergency affecting an employee, the People team will contact the person listed.
