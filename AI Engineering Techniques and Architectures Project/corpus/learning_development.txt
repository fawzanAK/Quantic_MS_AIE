POL-013 | Learning and Development Policy
Owner: People Operations - Talent | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Applies to: Full-time and part-time employees
================================================================

SECTION 1. PURPOSE

Northwind invests in the growth of its people. This policy describes the annual learning budget, tuition reimbursement, conference attendance, and the time employees may spend on learning during working hours.

SECTION 2. ELIGIBILITY

Full-time employees who have completed the 90-day introductory period are eligible for the learning budget and for tuition reimbursement as described below. Part-time employees are eligible for the learning budget in proportion to their scheduled hours. Contractors and interns are not eligible.

SECTION 3. ANNUAL LEARNING BUDGET

Each eligible employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year, which can be used for online courses, books, certifications, workshops, and professional memberships that relate to the employee's current role or career path at Northwind. Employees need manager approval before spending from the budget. The budget does not carry over to the next year. Expenses are submitted under POL-005 within 30 days of payment, with a receipt.

SECTION 4. TUITION REIMBURSEMENT FOR DEGREE PROGRAMS

Employees who have worked at Northwind for at least 12 months may apply for tuition reimbursement for part-time degree or certificate programs at an accredited institution. The conditions are:

  - The program must relate to the employee's current role or a planned future role at Northwind.
  - The employee must get written approval from their manager and the People team before enrolling.
  - Northwind reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year.
  - Reimbursement is paid after the course ends, once the employee provides proof of a final grade of B or better (or "pass" for pass/fail courses).
  - If the employee leaves Northwind within 12 months after receiving a reimbursement, they must repay the amount reimbursed for the previous 12 months, as local law allows.

SECTION 5. TIME FOR LEARNING

Employees may spend up to 4 hours per week of working time on learning activities that are agreed with their manager. Time for study toward a degree is normally outside working hours, but managers may allow flexible scheduling for classes that take place in working hours. Study days before exams can be taken as PTO under POL-001.

SECTION 6. CONFERENCES AND EXTERNAL EVENTS

Attending conferences is treated as business travel and follows POL-011 and the expense limits in POL-005. Conference attendance requires the manager's approval and, for international events, the Director's approval. Employees who attend a conference are expected to share what they learned with their team within 30 days.

SECTION 7. INTERNAL TRAINING AND MENTORING

Northwind runs internal workshops, leadership programs, and a mentoring program that matches employees with senior mentors for 6 months. Employees can sign up through the learning portal. Participation in mandatory compliance training (see POL-009 and POL-004) is not paid from the learning budget.

SECTION 8. QUESTIONS

Questions about the learning budget or tuition reimbursement should be sent to learning@northwind.example.
