POL-006: Employee Benefits Guide

Owner: People Operations — Benefits | Plan year: January 1 to December 31, 2026 | Applies to: All employees, subject to the eligibility rules below

1. Overview

Northwind offers a benefits program that protects the health and financial security of our employees and their families. This guide describes who is eligible for each benefit, what the company contributes, and when employees can make changes. The U.S. benefits described in Sections 3 to 8 apply to employees who are employed in the United States. Employees in Pakistan and the United Kingdom receive the local benefits described in Section 9.

2. Eligibility

  • Full-time employees (scheduled to work 35 or more hours per week) are eligible for all benefits. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after the employee's start date.
  • Part-time employees who are scheduled to work at least 20 hours per week are eligible for dental, vision, and the 401(k) plan (without the company match). They are not eligible for the company medical plans, the wellness stipend, or company-paid life insurance.
  • Employees who work fewer than 20 hours per week, independent contractors, interns, and temporary agency workers are not eligible for Northwind benefits.
  • Eligible dependents include a legal spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26.

New employees must enroll within 30 days of becoming eligible. If they do not enroll, they are treated as having waived coverage until the next open enrollment period, unless they have a qualifying life event.

3. Medical Plans

Eligible U.S. employees can choose between two medical plans, a PPO plan and a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) that can be paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). Northwind pays 80% of the monthly premium for the employee and 60% of the premium for covered dependents.

4. Dental and Vision

Dental and vision coverage are available to all eligible full-time and part-time employees. Northwind pays 75% of the employee premium for both plans. Routine preventive dental care is covered at 100%.

5. Retirement: 401(k) Plan

Eligible U.S. employees can contribute to the Northwind 401(k) plan after 30 days of employment. Full-time employees receive a company match of 100% of the first 4% of pay that they contribute. Part-time employees can contribute but do not receive the match. Company matching contributions are vested immediately.

6. Open Enrollment and Qualifying Life Events

The annual open enrollment period runs from November 1 to November 15 for coverage that begins on January 1. Outside open enrollment, an employee can change elections only after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage. The employee must report the event and submit changes within 30 days of the event.

7. Wellness Stipend

Full-time employees receive a wellness stipend of $50 per month that can be used for gym memberships, fitness apps, or mental health services. The stipend is taxable income in some jurisdictions and is paid through payroll.

8. Life and Disability Insurance

Full-time employees receive basic life and accidental death insurance equal to one times annual base salary at no cost. Short-term disability pays 60% of base pay for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day waiting period; details on leaves are in POL-007. Employees can buy additional life insurance for themselves and their dependents.

9. Benefits for Employees Outside the United States

Pakistan (Karachi office). Full-time employees are covered by a company-paid group health insurance plan that includes the employee, a spouse, and up to four children. Northwind also contributes 8% of base salary to the provident fund. Part-time employees receive pro-rated provident fund contributions but are not covered by the group health plan.

United Kingdom (London office). Full-time employees receive private medical insurance and an employer pension contribution of 5% of base salary in the workplace pension scheme. UK employees are also entitled to statutory benefits such as statutory sick pay and statutory parental leave pay.

The U.S. medical plans, HSA, 401(k), and wellness stipend described above are not available to employees who are employed outside the United States.

10. Benefits During Leave

During approved paid leave, benefits continue and the employee continues to pay their share of premiums through payroll. During unpaid leave, employees can continue medical coverage by paying the full premium. See POL-007 for leave details.

11. Questions

For questions about eligibility or enrollment, contact the Benefits team at benefits@northwind.example. Where this guide and the official plan documents differ, the plan documents control.
