# POL-010: Equipment and IT Asset Policy

**Owner:** IT Operations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees and contractors who use Northwind equipment

## 1. Purpose

Northwind provides the equipment that employees need to do their jobs. This policy explains what is provided, how to request or replace equipment, and what employees must do to protect it. Security requirements for devices are in POL-004.

## 2. Standard Equipment

Each full-time employee receives:

- one company laptop (Mac or Windows, depending on the role);
- one external monitor for hybrid and fully remote employees, or a desk monitor in the office;
- a headset, a keyboard, and a mouse; and
- software from the approved software catalog.

Customer Support employees also receive a dedicated phone headset. Part-time employees receive the same laptop and software. Contractors receive equipment only if their agreement says so.

## 3. Laptop Refresh Cycle

Laptops are replaced every **36 months**. Employees may ask for an earlier replacement if the laptop no longer meets the needs of the role, if there are repeated hardware failures, or if it is damaged and cannot be repaired. IT will confirm eligibility before ordering a new device. Older laptops must be returned to IT and wiped before disposal.

## 4. Requesting Equipment

Employees request new, replacement, or additional equipment by creating a ticket with the IT Service Desk. Standard items are normally delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Non-standard requests require manager approval and may require a business case. Employees must not purchase laptops or other computing devices on their own and ask for reimbursement; such purchases are not reimbursable under POL-005, Section 7.

## 5. Ergonomic Support

Employees can request a free virtual ergonomic assessment from IT and People Operations. Furniture, such as chairs and desks, is not provided for home offices, but eligible employees can use the home office stipend described in POL-005, Section 6.

## 6. Personal Devices

Employees must not use personal laptops to work with Confidential or Restricted data. Employees may use a personal phone to access email and chat if it is enrolled in mobile device management (MDM) and meets the security requirements in POL-004. Northwind may remotely wipe the work data on a personal phone, but not the personal data.

## 7. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Equipment

If equipment is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the Security team **within 1 hour of discovering the loss** and to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours (see POL-004, Section 7). If the loss occurred outside the home country, the employee should also contact the local police and send IT a copy of the report. Employees who damage equipment by accident should tell IT as soon as possible. Repeated or careless damage may result in the employee being asked to contribute to the repair cost.

## 8. Travel With Equipment

Laptops may travel only to countries on the approved list in POL-004, Section 4, unless the CISO has approved an exception. For high-risk destinations, IT can provide a temporary loaner laptop. Equipment must be kept in carry-on luggage and must not be left unattended in vehicles or hotel rooms.

## 9. Return of Equipment

When an employee leaves Northwind or changes roles, all equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of the separation date. IT sends a prepaid shipping kit to remote employees. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from final pay where local law allows or may be recovered by other lawful means.

## 10. Software and Accounts

Employees may install only software from the approved catalog. Requests for new software are submitted to IT and reviewed by Security. Unlicensed or pirated software is prohibited.
