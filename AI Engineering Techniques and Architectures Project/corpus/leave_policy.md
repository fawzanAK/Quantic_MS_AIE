# POL-007: Leave of Absence Policy

**Owner:** People Operations | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Applies to:** All employees, subject to local law

## 1. Overview

Northwind recognizes that employees sometimes need extended time away from work because of illness, family needs, or civic duties. This policy describes the paid and unpaid leaves available, who is eligible, and the steps to request them. Where local law in the United States (federal, state, or city), Pakistan, or the United Kingdom provides more generous rights, the law applies. Annual vacation time is covered separately in POL-001.

## 2. Sick Leave

Full-time employees receive **10 paid sick days per calendar year**, available from the first day of employment. Part-time employees receive sick days in proportion to their scheduled hours. Sick days can be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or to care for a sick family member. A medical certificate is required for absences of **more than 3 consecutive working days**. Sick days do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out at termination. Sick leave is separate from PTO; employees should not use PTO for sick days unless the sick balance is used up.

## 3. Parental Leave

Northwind offers paid parental leave to employees after they have completed the 90-day introductory period:

- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base pay.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 8 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base pay.

Leave can start at any time within 12 months of a birth or adoption or foster placement and must be taken in one or two blocks. The employee should give at least **30 days' notice** when the event is foreseeable and tell their manager and the People team. Benefits continue during paid parental leave (see POL-006). Parental leave runs together with any legally required leave such as FMLA, and does not extend it.

## 4. Family and Medical Leave (United States)

Eligible employees in the United States may take up to **12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave** in a 12-month period under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). To be eligible, the employee must have worked for Northwind for at least 12 months, have worked at least 1,250 hours in the last 12 months, and work at a location where Northwind has 50 or more employees within 75 miles. Qualifying reasons include the employee's own serious health condition, the birth or placement of a child, and care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition. Employees must give 30 days' notice when the need is foreseeable. Health benefits continue during FMLA leave on the same terms as for active employees.

## 5. Medical Leave and Short-Term Disability

Employees who cannot work because of their own illness, injury, or recovery from childbirth may be eligible for short-term disability (STD) benefits. STD pays **60% of base pay for up to 12 weeks**, after a **7-day waiting period**. Employees may use their sick days and PTO to cover the waiting period. Employees should send a medical certificate to the People team and may be asked to update it periodically.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

- **Immediate family** (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or the same relations of a spouse or partner): up to **5 paid days**.
- **Extended family and close friends:** up to **3 paid days**.

Additional time off can be requested as PTO. Managers should handle requests with sensitivity and should not ask for proof beyond what is needed for payroll.

## 7. Jury Duty and Civic Obligations

Employees who are called for jury duty or to testify as a witness receive their normal pay for up to **10 working days**. Employees should show their summons to their manager as soon as it is received. Time spent voting, if not possible outside working hours, is covered as required by local law.

## 8. Military Leave

Employees who serve in a military reserve or national guard unit are entitled to unpaid military leave, and re-employment rights, in line with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) in the United States and equivalent local laws elsewhere. Northwind provides paid leave of up to 10 working days per year for required annual training.

## 9. How to Request Leave

1. Tell your manager and the People team as early as possible, in writing.
2. Submit the leave request in the HR system with the leave type, expected dates, and any required documents.
3. The People team confirms eligibility, pay, and benefits within 5 business days.
4. Keep your manager updated about your return date. Employees returning from leave of more than 4 weeks meet with their manager to plan their return.

## 10. Pay, PTO Accrual, and Job Protection

PTO continues to accrue during paid leave. During unpaid leave of more than 30 days, PTO does not accrue. Employees on FMLA or other legally protected leave return to the same or an equivalent position. Employees are not penalized for taking leave they are entitled to take, and Northwind does not retaliate against employees who use leave.
